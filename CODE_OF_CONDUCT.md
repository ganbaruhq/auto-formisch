# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and considerate, welcome people of every background and experience level, give and take feedback gracefully, and focus on what's best for the project and its users. Harassment, insults, and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, contact the maintainers of the [ganbaruhq](https://github.com/ganbaruhq) organization privately. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, or block people who violate this code, as described in the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
